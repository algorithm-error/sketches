Range slider

Like the slider, but with two handles: a low from and a high to on one track, with the span between them filled. Handles can meet but never cross — drag one past the other and it stops at its neighbour. Dragging the filled span itself slides the whole range, keeping its length, until one handle reaches an end. Built from two stacked native range inputs, so each handle keeps native keyboard support.

Basic

Fires input continuously while dragging and change once the drag ends; both carry detail.from and detail.to.
