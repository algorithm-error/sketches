Compound range slider

A label, a help line, a range slider, and a number field per handle, composed into one control — the two-handle counterpart of the compound slider. Use it instead of two separate sliders whenever the pair is really one span.

Basic

Dragging a handle, dragging the filled span to slide the whole range, or typing in either field keeps everything in sync and dispatches one change event with detail.from and detail.to. Each field clamps against the other, so the range can't invert.

2 – 500

Decimals

decimals sets how the number fields format their value, the same as on compound slider.
